STREAMLOCK V8 — PROFESSIONAL CUSTOM PLAYER & LOCKED SPONSOR ADS

PROFESSIONAL PLAYER INTERFACE
- Rebuilt the controls as arranged overlays floating above the video.
- Top overlay: StreamLock branding, quality selector, and fullscreen.
- Bottom overlay: seek timeline, elapsed time, duration, play/pause, mute,
  volume slider, and playback-speed selector.
- Small, aligned controls with responsive desktop/mobile sizing.
- Controls fade and slide away after playback inactivity. Moving, touching,
  or clicking the player reveals them without unexpectedly pausing playback.
- A dedicated center play button controls paused playback.
- Persistent source masks and blocked iframe touch controls keep external
  native controls behind the StreamLock interface. External hosts may still
  impose their own embedding or playback restrictions.

GENUINE PLAYBACK AND QUALITY
- Real timeline, elapsed time, duration, seeking, play/pause, volume, mute,
  and speed for direct/uploaded videos.
- YouTube and Vimeo playback APIs supply external time, duration, state,
  volume, seeking, speed, and quality commands where supported.
- Quality menu: Auto, HD, 1080p, 720p, 480p, and 360p.
- HD and 1080p remain rewarded; lower resolutions are free.
- Developers can upload actual files for every listed resolution.
- YouTube/Vimeo quality options request the corresponding real stream from
  the provider; the provider selects the closest genuinely available stream.
- No URL-only resolution labels or simulated uploaded renditions.

SPONSOR AD BEHAVIOR
- Fullscreen rewarded ads never force or lock screen rotation.
- Sponsor videos have no adjustable resolution or native control access.
- Direct sponsor videos resist pause, seeking, speed/volume changes,
  picture-in-picture, remote playback, downloads, and context menus.
- External sponsor touch controls are blocked; tapping visits the ad link.
- Banner sponsor videos use the same locked playback behavior.
- Ordinary sponsor taps never grant rewards; Check Offer remains explicit.
