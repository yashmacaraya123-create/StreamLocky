HOW TO USE THE STREAMLOCK LICENSE

The included LICENSE.txt is a proprietary license. It reserves ownership and
requires permission before other people may use, copy, modify, host, sell, or
redistribute StreamLock. It does not make your project open source.

QUICK SETUP
1. Open LICENSE.txt and confirm the copyright owner and year. It currently
   says: Copyright © 2026 Yash Macaraya.
2. Keep LICENSE.txt in StreamLock's main folder whenever you share, sell, or
   deploy the project.
3. If you authorize a client or customer, give them written terms identifying:
   - their name or company;
   - approved domain names and number of installations;
   - whether modifications are allowed;
   - whether resale, sublicensing, and client deployments are allowed;
   - the license period, payment terms, support, and termination rules.
4. Save proof of your original work, such as dated source archives, commits,
   project notes, and deployment records.
5. Add this short notice near the top of important original source files if
   desired:

   Copyright © 2026 Yash Macaraya. All rights reserved.
   StreamLock proprietary software. See LICENSE.txt.

IMPORTANT LIMITS
- A copyright notice does not give you ownership of third-party videos,
  advertisements, images, trademarks, APIs, or embedded platform content.
  Obtain separate permission for content you do not own.
- Uploading a project to GitHub or deploying it to Netlify does not by itself
  make it open source. The license determines what others may do.
- If you want others to reuse the code freely, use an open-source license such
  as MIT instead. Do not apply both licenses to the same distribution without
  clearly explaining which files and permissions each license covers.
- This document is a practical template, not legal advice. For commercial
  licensing, enforcement, or a customized agreement, consult a qualified
  lawyer in the jurisdiction that applies to you and your customers.
